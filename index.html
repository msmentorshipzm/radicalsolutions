The Vision

Bridging Technical Expertise with Youth Innovation

I am Milimo Siabana, your Radical Engineer and a Mining Engineering Student at UNZA. My mission is to cultivate a new generation of Zambian and African problem-solvers who combine hands-on technical discipline with bold strategic vision.

Through video content, engineering discussions, tech breakdowns, and essay challenges, this portal serves as an incubator for practical solutions that directly support the Grow Zambia Agenda and national industrialization. Once the solutions have been suggested from the Essays, the most feasible ideas and indeed the winner will be invited to be a part of the Radical Engineers and make prototypes for the same, and pitch the idea, with the already working prototype, to the relevant institutions for possible consideration.

Engineering Methodology

From Concept to Working Prototype

Every proposal evaluated on this platform is judged against rigorous engineering standards designed to move ideas off paper and into Zambian industry.

Governance & Academic Standards

Submission Integrity, Contest Rules & Guide

Review our academic integrity standards, official competition rules, and step-by-step submission instructions before uploading your manuscript.

Permitted Research & Drafting Tools

  • Using digital tools to gather publicly available industrial statistics, ZESCO grid figures, or agricultural production data with proper citations.
  • Structuring engineering calculations, cost estimates, and technical outlines prior to authoring your original prose.
  • Performing post-draft spelling and punctuation checks while preserving your authentic technical voice.

Academic Disqualification Criteria

  • Zero-Tolerance AI Generation: Automated AI-detection screening is active on all submissions; AI-generated manuscripts are automatically disqualified.
  • Plagiarism & Uncited Work: Reproducing text from journals, policy papers, or articles without explicit academic attribution.
  • Format & Word Bounds: Manuscripts outside the strict 750–1,500 word window or mismatched between the uploaded document and pasted body text.
