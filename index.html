The Vision

Bridging Technical Expertise with Youth Innovation

I am Milimo Siabana, your Radical Engineer and a Mining Engineering Student at UNZA. My mission is to cultivate a new generation of Zambian and African problem-solvers who combine hands-on technical discipline with bold strategic vision.

Through video content, engineering discussions, tech breakdowns, and essay challenges, this portal serves as an incubator for practical solutions that directly support the Grow Zambia Agenda and national industrialization. Once the solutions have been suggested from the Essays, the most feasible ideas and indeed the winners will be invited to be a part of the Radical Engineers and make prototypes for the same, and pitch the idea, with the already working prototype, to the relevant institutions for possible consideration.

Submission Integrity

Ethical AI Policy & Writing Guidelines

Permitted AI Usage (Research)

  • Brainstorming initial perspectives or structural outlines.
  • Researching data points, statistics, and industrial references.
  • Checking grammar and sentence structure after writing.

Strictly Forbidden (Zero Tolerance)

  • WARNING: AI detectors are incorporated in the submission portal. Attach both the PDF or Word Document and paste your whole Essay in the space provided.
  • Copying and pasting fully AI-generated prose or essays.
  • Submitting plagiarized content from existing journals or blogs.
  • Exceeding the strict maximum limit of 1,500 words.
